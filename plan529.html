Educational estimate only. Returns are hypothetical and not guaranteed; a 529 is investment-based and can lose value. Qualified distributions for eligible education expenses are generally federal income-tax-free; nonqualified distributions can make the earnings portion taxable and may trigger a separate 10% additional federal tax unless an exception applies. State tax treatment, deductions and credits differ by state and often apply only to that state's own plan — several states offer none at all. The taxable comparison applies the entered rate to the growth each year as a simplified annual drag; it is not a tax-return calculation and real brokerage tax depends on turnover, holding periods and the type of gain. Contribution limits, gift-tax rules and financial-aid treatment are not modelled. Confirm with a qualified tax and financial professional.
