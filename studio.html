      Why most questions cost nothing

      Every tool answers what it can on its own — definitions, what-ifs, solving for a figure, what stands out. Those are measured off the calculator in front of you and never leave the page. Only a question the page cannot place is sent on, and even then the model is not allowed to produce a figure of its own: it asks the calculator to run the numbers and quotes what comes back.
