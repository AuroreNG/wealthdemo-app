    The cards above measure every goal against the same untouched balance, which is how the original calculator works and is the right way to see whether each goal is reachable on its own. This panel does the other half: it takes each goal out in the year it arrives, so the later goals are measured against what would really be left.

    Educational estimate only. Estimated Accumulated Value is calculated from the monthly amount and annual growth rate entered by the user, compounded monthly. It is not a life-insurance carrier illustration, guaranteed cash value, dividend or credit, or representation of actual policy performance. Your goals don’t all happen at retirement; this roadmap helps you visualise how consistent saving and a hypothetical growth assumption compare with the goals you want to accomplish throughout your financial life. Taxes, fees, policy charges and loan interest are not modelled, and no withdrawal is assumed in the cards above. Confirm with a qualified financial professional.
