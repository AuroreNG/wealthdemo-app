How this works

Seven short steps. Nothing is sent anywhere — everything stays in this browser.

  • One question at a timeAnswer it, press Continue, and it folds into a summary. The next question opens.
  • Change anything, anytimePress Edit on any answered question to reopen it. Your numbers update everywhere at once.
  • Stuck on a question?Press the i beside it for a plain-language explanation and an example.
  • It reads as you goSmart Insights ranks what matters in your numbers, and Ask answers questions about them — all on this device.
