How accurate is 72 ÷ rate?—

It is a shortcut, not the exact answer. It is spot on at 7.85%. Below that it says doubling takes a little longer than it really does; above that, a little less. Tap any rate to try it.

Use 70For rates under 4%.
Use 72For 4–12% — most saving and investing.
Use 76For 18% and up — credit cards.
